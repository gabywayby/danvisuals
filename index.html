Booking Policies

Reserve your date

Your date is reserved once availability, package, and reservation payment are agreed. I’ll confirm the amount and due date with you before booking.

Changes & cancellations

Please get in touch as soon as possible if your plans change. Rescheduling depends on availability, and any payment or cancellation terms will follow what we agree on when booking.

Photo delivery

All packages include delivery through Google Drive. Wedding packages also include a free USB. Edited photos are delivered within the turnaround listed in your selected package.

Travel

If your venue requires a travel fee, I’ll let you know the amount before your booking is confirmed.
